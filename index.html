EightBells

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 17, 2026

EightBells is designed to run on your device and ring scheduled ship's bells. The app does not require an account, and it is not designed to collect, sell, or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

EightBells stores the following information locally on your device:

  • whether EightBells is enabled
  • your chosen alert mode
  • your first-bell and quiet-hours schedule
  • your bell volume and vibration intensity settings
  • whether you have completed onboarding

How the app uses this information

This information is used only to:

  • remember your preferences
  • schedule bells at the times you selected
  • restore bell scheduling after reboot or app update

Permissions used

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: used to show a silent running-status notification and a stop action
  • VIBRATE: used when you choose vibration alerts
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: used to improve half-hour timing accuracy
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: used to restore bell scheduling after a device restart or app update

Data sharing

EightBells does not send personal data to the developer, to analytics providers, or to advertising services.

Children

EightBells is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if app features or data practices change.
